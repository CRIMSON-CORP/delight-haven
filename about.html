Meet Our Manager

Feyisayo Olajide is the Founder and Managing Director of Delight Haven Assisted Living, a compassionate leader with extensive experience in healthcare and senior care services. She is known for her commitment to excellence, dignity in care, and creating environments where seniors feel truly at home.

With 9 years of hands-on experience supporting older adults, Feyisayo developed a deep understanding of the physical, emotional, and personal needs that come with aging. Her professional journey allowed her to witness both the challenges families face when seeking quality care and the life-changing impact of compassionate, well-structured support. These experiences inspired her to establish Delight Haven Assisted Living — a refined, homelike community designed to provide personalized care, comfort, and peace of mind.

Her leadership combines clinical knowledge, strong operational oversight, and a warm, person-centered approach. Feyisayo believes that exceptional senior living should balance safety, wellness, and elegance, ensuring that every resident is treated with respect and individuality. Under her direction, Delight Haven reflects her vision of premium care — where professionalism meets compassion, and where residents experience not just assistance, but belonging, purpose, and a higher standard of living.
